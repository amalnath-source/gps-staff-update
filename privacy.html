GPS HSE Department — Privacy

These pages (the Project HSE Staff Update and the HSE Competency Assessment) are internal tools of the HSE Department of Gulf Petrochemical Services & Trading LLC (GPS), Oman.

What we use from Google sign-in

Only your name and e-mail address, to confirm who you are and which project you may update. We do not read your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.

What we keep

What you submit (staff status, work patterns, leave plans and applications, assessment answers and attachments) is stored in GPS Google Sheets and Google Drive, for HSE staff management only. It is not sold or shared outside GPS.
